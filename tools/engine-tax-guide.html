Engine size & tax guide

Token / motor vehicle tax in Pakistan is commonly grouped by engine capacity (cc) and vehicle type. Find your band here, then confirm the live amount on the official portal.

Typical car / jeep bands

Engine capacityNotes
Up to 1000ccUsually the lowest private-car token band
1001–1300ccCommon hatchback / sedan range
1301–1500ccMid-size engines; higher annual dues
1501–2000ccOften a clear step up in token tax
2001–2500ccHigher capacity private vehicles
Above 2500ccHighest common private bands

Motorcycles

Many provinces treat smaller motorcycles with lifetime token at registration, while larger bikes can have different schedules. Always re-check the official record before buying or transferring.

Important

  • Exact rupee amounts change by province and budget year.
  • Rebates for early or digital payment are announced officially when available.
  • This page is an educational band finder — not an official tax invoice.
Check or pay on official channels

Use your province Excise / e-Pay portal for the current amount due.
