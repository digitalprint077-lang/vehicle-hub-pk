Punjab Vehicle Verification — Check Details Online

Learn how to confirm a vehicle's record before you buy, transfer, or put a commercial vehicle on the road. This guide covers ownership checks on the official Excise MTMIS portal.

GuideUpdated for public online checks~6 min read
Quick actions

Use official portals when a deal involves money.

Official MTMIS Track Smart Card

What vehicle verification means in Punjab

Vehicle verification is a quick online check against government-linked records. On the official Excise MTMIS portal you can usually see owner identity fields, engine and chassis numbers, and token-tax related clues for vehicles registered in Punjab.

Advertisement

How to verify on official MTMIS (step by step)

  1. Open the official portal at mtmis.excise.punjab.gov.pk.
  2. Enter the registration number exactly as printed on the plate when possible.
  3. Complete the captcha / security check.
  4. Submit the search and review owner, vehicle, and payment-related fields.
  5. Match engine and chassis numbers to the physical vehicle before paying any deposit.

If the first format fails, try with and without dashes or spaces. Punjab plates are stored in more than one pattern.

SMS option

When you have no internet, many users send the registration number by SMS to 8785 for a basic reply (standard SMS charges apply). The web portal remains better for a full on-screen record.

Why this check matters before buying

  • Mismatched engine/chassis numbers are a hard stop.
  • Old owner names may mean an incomplete transfer (open-letter risk).
  • Unpaid dues can follow the vehicle to the next owner.
  • Pending application status can mean paperwork is not finished yet.

Common problems

  • Not found: retry plate formats; confirm the province of registration.
  • Old owner name: formal transfer may never have been completed.
  • New vehicle missing: fresh records can take time to appear.
  • Portal busy: try again off-peak or use SMS for a basic check.

FAQ

Is online verification free?

Public web checks are generally free. SMS may carry network charges. Official transfers and cards involve government fees.

Can I search by CNIC on the public portal?

Public MTMIS-style tools are built around registration numbers, not open CNIC browsing, for privacy reasons.

Does MTMIS cover Islamabad or Karachi vehicles?

No. Punjab MTMIS covers Punjab-registered vehicles. Other regions use their own systems.

Other registration regions

Punjab MTMIS does not show a vehicle registered in another province or in Islamabad. Use the portal that matches the plate. These steps use the official addresses already listed on this site.

Sindh

  1. Open the official vehicle search: excise.gos.pk/vehicle/vehicle_search.
  2. Search with the registration number. The Sindh portal covers 4-wheelers and 2-wheelers registered in Sindh, including Karachi.
  3. To look up vehicles linked to a CNIC, use search by CNIC.
  4. Match the result to the vehicle before you pay a deposit. The helpline published on the Sindh portal is (021) 111 374 634.

Islamabad

ICT records are separate from Punjab. Islamabad Excise says to use islamabadexcise.gov.pk and the CITY ISLAMABAD app.

  1. Start at that official site, then open the vehicle-detail tool it lists: check vehicle detail.
  2. Use the registration number from the plate and read the result on that page.
  3. Transfer, smart card, and challan tools are grouped on Islamabad Excise.

Balochistan

  1. Open Balochistan online vehicle verification.
  2. If that tool is down, retry later from excise.balochistan.gov.pk.

Gilgit-Baltistan

  1. Open gbexcise.gov.pk/vehicle-search.
  2. Search the GB record there. Phone numbers published on the portal are (05811) 920990 and 920393.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

This site does not list a KPK equivalent of Punjab MTMIS. The Dastak home at dastak.kp.gov.pk is digital arms licensing, not a vehicle-ownership search. For a KPK driving licence, use the licence guide. For registration questions, start from the KPK page and the official Excise channel the province publishes.
