E-Smart Card (Vehicle Registration Card) in Punjab

Punjab has moved many vehicle records from the old paper book toward a chip-based smart card with a QR code for roadside checks.

What the smart card is for

The card stores key registration identity for the vehicle. Traffic police can use the QR / digital record to confirm details quickly. After a new registration or ownership transfer, printing and delivery can take time.

Advertisement

How to track smart-card status

  1. Keep your application / chassis references from Excise.
  2. Use the official Excise / MTMIS tracking options published for smart-card status.
  3. Watch for statuses such as printing, dispatched, or ready for collection (wording varies by portal update).
  4. Do not pay agents who promise "instant cards" outside official channels.
E-Smart Card portal Track Smart Card

Tips while waiting for a card

  • Keep payment receipts and application numbers safe.
  • Verify the vehicle online while the physical card is printing.
  • If status stalls for too long, visit the relevant Excise office with original documents — or check the district directory.

Smart cards in other regions

Islamabad

  1. Open the status tool listed by Islamabad Excise: smart card status.
  2. The department home is islamabadexcise.gov.pk. The same tools are listed on Islamabad Excise.

Balochistan

Balochistan Excise shows smart card verification as under process. There is no live status URL on this site yet. Use excise.balochistan.gov.pk.

Sindh

Sindh does not have a separate VRC site listed here. Registration checks use Sindh vehicle search.

Gilgit-Baltistan

No separate GB smart-card tracker is listed. Use gbexcise.gov.pk.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

No KPK vehicle smart-card tracker is listed. The Dastak portal is arms licensing, not a vehicle card tracker.

FAQ

Is the paper book still valid?

Punjab has shifted many records to the e-smart card. Carry whatever Excise currently recognises for your vehicle, and use official tracking if a new card was ordered after transfer or registration.

Where do I track delivery?

Start with the official Pakistan Post / Excise tracking link and the VRC / e-smart card portal.
